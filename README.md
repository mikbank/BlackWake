# Blackwake — Free Roam

For a source tour and the TypeScript conventions used here, start with [CODE_GUIDE.md](CODE_GUIDE.md).

Personal browser space prototype. Click/tap destinations, dock at stations, trade cargo, and use outer jump zones to travel through a thirteen-system network. Twenty NPCs from the Frontier and Aster Compact include assigned-route and roaming long-haul freighters, public transports, multi-system leisure liners and police patrols, with travel across a thirteen-system network. Reach Connector runs public passenger services, Aurora Leisure tours several systems and returns to Haven, and Marshal Seven patrols Kepler Drift and responds to nearby attack orders. Their actual activities produce text bubbles over the speaking ships. Civilian traffic is neutral; police may intervene when you attack nearby ships. Broadside combat, physical burst PDCs, boarding, ship capture, the compact HUD and live minimap remain available.

`npm install`, then `npm run dev`. `npm run build` type-checks and builds. `npm test` checks simulation, trading, travel, persistence and extension boundaries.

New games start in free roam, docked at Haven with 500 credits and no assigned mission. Traffic has already lived through five minutes of simulated journeys, so ships are underway throughout the network; existing saves resume their own timeline. For an optional trading route, open the commodity market and buy rations for 12 cr; undock and approach the outer jump zone. Plot a route to Kepler Drift on the universe map, then use the local jump menu, dock at Relay and sell for 22 cr. Buy machine parts there for 26 cr and sell them at Haven for 45 cr. You can trade, explore, or attack traffic freely; completing this route is not required.

Select any NPC, open Contact details and choose **Follow this ship** to inspect its role/manifest and observe its job. At the outer gate, jump manually to follow a travelling ship into the next system; the same identity is reacquired there. Stop or a new destination cancels following. Menus and docking pause the game clock.

Tap a station or gate on the system map to set a course. Tap a contact in space to select it. Selecting a neutral ship exposes Attack directly in flight. The contextual button also offers approach, dock, jump or board when appropriate. Boarding offers Raid cargo (keep your current hull) or Take ship (capture the vessel and its cargo, leaving your previous hull as salvage). Transfers respect capacity and commodity sizes; overflow remains aboard the original hull. Police witnesses treat boarding as aggression, and pursuers remain hostile after you change hulls. The minimap opens the local chart; the system name opens the universe map. Menu contains ship/cargo details, weapons, help, sound and save options. `S` or Space stops; `E` uses the contextual action; Escape pauses.

Menu → Raid test opens the earlier encounter as a separate saved scenario. Menu → Free roam returns to the voyage, retaining the existing browser save slot. Older saved trading guides keep their progress; new games have no guide attached. Earlier version-1 raid saves are preserved and migrated into the raid slot. Progress saves to IndexedDB every five seconds and on successful commands, with explicit fallback/status and JSON export/import. Version-1/2/3/4/5 saves migrate automatically to schema 6; original browser keys are retained. Saves belong to this browser and site address. Export backups before clearing browser data.

See [TRAFFIC_JOBS.md](TRAFFIC_JOBS.md) for registering new NPC policies and [CULTURES.md](CULTURES.md) for culture, hull design and radio profiles. Ashen and Kepler use Frontier defaults; Vesper is home to the Aster Compact, with cultural visitors and distinct role silhouettes. NPC culture and hull design persist independently across system travel, and capture preserves the foreign hull. See [SYSTEM_AUTHORING.md](SYSTEM_AUTHORING.md) for editable system files, seeded generation commands, paired gates and route planning. New themes, runtime generation and shipyards are not implemented.

Open the universe map and plot a route to Vesper to visit Lantern Anchorage. Ashen has separate exits for Kepler and Vesper; from Kepler, the plotted route passes through Ashen. Each jump remains manual. The original Haven–Relay trading circuit still works.

For longer flights, set a course and tap **Cruise** or press **C**. It spools for 1.5 seconds, reaches 4× normal speed, and slows automatically near the destination. Tap it again or use Stop to disengage. Nearby hostiles/incoming fire block cruise; damage adds a short lockout. Broadsides hold during spool/cruise while PDCs continue defending. The current slice enables player cruise; NPC travel policies retain their existing speeds. Cruise activation resets when stopping, opening menus, jumping or reloading; the voyage save remains compatible.

The Aster Compact is based in Vesper, with a visiting commuter in Ashen. Ship roles have distinct hulls, and Aster radio appears as text bubbles over ships. Select a contact to rotate its model. See [CULTURES.md](CULTURES.md) for the live fleet, role design pools and save continuity.

Lacuna, Halcyon, Nacre, Ilex, Meridian and Brume extend the Aster **Lantern Marches** beyond Vesper. The generator accepts `--names` and `--region`, inheriting region culture independently of the layout preset; see [SYSTEM_AUTHORING.md](SYSTEM_AUTHORING.md) for the replayable command.

The universe map groups destinations by region and previews one selected route. Plotting sets the next exit course. At that exit, the separate jump menu lists only currently available direct destinations.

Markets now offer 21 goods with station-specific assortments and separate **Buy goods** / **Sell cargo** views. Sell shows unsupported cargo as well as accepted goods; category filters and per-item Max controls help manage the hold. Luxury liners carry real valuable provisions. See [MARKETS.md](MARKETS.md) for quotes, generation and save continuity.

The first 3D presentation test is live: perspective chase camera, volumetric stations/gates, textured planets and a surrounding star field. Flight and combat remain on a plane. Switch **Menu → Camera** or press **V** for the tactical view. Tap below the horizon to plot a course, or use the retained maps for distant contacts. The spatial simulation now uses a main-thread ECS; [ECS.md](ECS.md) explains the component/record bridge and deferred worker direction.

System jumps now enter a four-second hyperspace sequence while the destination prepares. Slow loading holds the corridor without consuming extra game time; failed arrival offers Retry or Return to departure. Background NPC journeys advance by the same fixed interval. Closing during travel restores the departure checkpoint. See [TRAVEL.md](TRAVEL.md) for the async boundary and future streaming direction.

Travel points are now industrial hyperspace gateways. Jump visuals accelerate through a forward star stream, enter a curved wormhole, then return to visible stars that slow to points. Slow loads hold in the wormhole until the exit is ready. Gate IDs, routes and existing saves remain compatible.

The thirteen systems now demonstrate six space environments: Frontier haze, Violet veil, Amber dust, Ion blue, Emerald rift and Deep void. Maps show the profile name and physical radius. Appearance, infrastructure colors, culture and system size are independent; environment selection does not resize systems. The creator supports fixed profiles, seeded profile pools and size/radius overrides. Run `npm run universe:environments` or read [ENVIRONMENTS.md](ENVIRONMENTS.md).

Local distances and system boundaries are now 5× larger; stations and docking envelopes are 3× larger. Ships keep their dimensions, speeds and weapon ranges. Use Cruise for longer routes. Existing saves upgrade their coordinates once while retaining cargo, damage and NPC journeys. See [SCALE.md](SCALE.md).
